Reading the name

Letters and words in a product name tell you what it is intended for.

01 · OPTIMAL

Our signature line

Distinguished by bioavailability, potency, or synergistic ingredients. Optimal Prenatal, Optimal Electrolyte, Optimal Multivitamin.

02 · ACTIVE

Bioactive forms

Bioactive nutrients in a specialized delivery format, typically lozenges for absorption. Active B12 5000, Active B12 1000.

03 · MF

Methyl-free

Free from methylated nutrients, for people who are sensitive to them. Optimal Prenatal MF, B Complex MF.
